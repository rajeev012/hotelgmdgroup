About JMD Group of Hotels

Premier hospitality, refined comfort and unrivaled convenience across Delhi's airport hub.

Our Legacy & Journey

Hospitality Rooted in Care & Excellence

The JMD Group of Hotels has established itself as an esteemed and recognized hospitality group across the Mahipalpur and Aerocity corridor of New Delhi. Strategically positioned just minutes from Indira Gandhi International Airport (T1, T2 & T3), JMD Group caters to thousands of global travellers, business executives, transit guests, and visiting families each month.

Every property in our collection reflects our core promise: immaculate cleanliness, contemporary amenities, delicious multi-cuisine dining, seamless high-speed connectivity, and warm traditional Indian hospitality. From complimentary airport shuttle services to flexible check-in options, our team is dedicated to making every stay comfortable and stress-free.
